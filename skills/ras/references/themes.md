# Built-in themes

The reference engine ships six theme packs. Choose based on communication
intent, not personal preference:

- `minimal` — clear, restrained, highly legible briefs.
- `editorial` — warm, considered analysis and executive narratives.
- `data-forward` — metric-heavy KPI, operating, and performance reports.
- `institutional` — formal assessments, audits, compliance, and governance.
- `technical` — architecture, implementation, incident, and engineering work.
- `narrative` — human-centered case studies, investigations, and field notes.

The packs are available as portable assets under `assets/themes/` and as
compiled runtime tokens. A project brand overlays the selected pack. The
engine remains compatible with v1 manifests that only declare
`style_decision.visual_style`.
