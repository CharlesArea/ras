# Layout recipes

Page composition follows intent:

- Executive summaries open with a clear cover band, then a compact summary
  panel, supported finding, and action panel.
- KPI and data reports prioritize the chart or metric deck, use dense tables,
  and keep labels close to the evidence.
- Technical reports use a blueprint cover, diagram-first sections, and
  implementation callouts.
- Institutional reports use numbered sections, formal source registers, and
  stable headers/footers.
- Editorial and narrative reports use asymmetric or chapter-like openings,
  warmer surfaces, and more visible section rhythm.

When a block cannot fit without clipping or an orphaned heading, preserve the
component boundary and move the complete component to the next page.
