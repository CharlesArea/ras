# Outputs and quality gates

The canonical model renders DOCX and PDF. A completed report must open without
repair warnings, preserve order/content/citations across formats, contain no
unresolved placeholders, avoid clipping and orphaned headings, caption and
describe every informative visual, meet accessible contrast targets, resolve
all sourced claims, and record the selected style, assumptions, omissions,
research, renderer versions, and QA results in the manifest.
