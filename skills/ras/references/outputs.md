# Outputs and quality gates

The canonical model renders DOCX and PDF, with an optional editable PPTX
presentation companion. A completed report must open without repair warnings,
preserve order/content/citations across requested formats, contain no unresolved
placeholders, avoid clipping and orphaned headings, caption and describe every
informative visual, meet accessible contrast targets, resolve all sourced
claims, and record the selected style, assumptions, omissions, research,
renderer versions, and QA results in the manifest. PPTX charts and tables should
remain native/editable, while source references and full block text belong in
speaker notes.
