# Options and trade-offs

| Path | Near-term cost | Service risk | Long-term position | Readout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Extend current stack | Lower | Lower | Fragmentation remains | Fastest stabilization, limited consolidation [S1] |
| Consolidate now | Higher | Higher | Stronger operating foundation | More change at once, higher execution risk [S1] |
| Phased discovery and migration | Moderate | Manageable | Improves position in steps | Preferred balance of learning and continuity [S1] |

The choice is less about finding a perfect platform immediately and more about reducing uncertainty before committing to a broad migration.
