# Executive summary

The recommended path is to approve a time-boxed discovery phase for a consolidated customer support platform, followed by a staged migration if the service and cost assumptions are confirmed [S1]. This approach addresses fragmented case visibility while limiting the operational risk of a single cutover.

## Decision ask

Approve discovery, appoint a business owner, and return with a migration decision after the first service and integration checks are complete.
