# Recommendation and decision ask

Approve a six-week discovery phase with three gates:

- confirm service-level and integration requirements;
- test the highest-risk workflow with representative cases; and
- produce a migration cost, sequencing, and ownership plan.

The leadership team should treat the discovery result as the next decision point, not as automatic approval for a full replacement [S1].
