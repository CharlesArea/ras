# Executive summary

The strategy is to make customer operations easier to enter, easier to learn from, and easier to manage. It prioritizes a stable foundation in Q1, a controlled pilot in Q2, and measured scale in Q3 [S1].

The roadmap deliberately sequences change so that each phase produces evidence for the next decision.
