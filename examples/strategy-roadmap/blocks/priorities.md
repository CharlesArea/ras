# Strategic priorities

1. **Simplify intake:** make the first customer and agent interaction easier to route and resolve.
2. **Improve knowledge reuse:** capture proven answers so the team does not solve the same problem from scratch.
3. **Make service health visible:** establish a shared operating view of response time, backlog, and self-service.

These priorities are mutually reinforcing: simpler intake creates cleaner learning signals, and better visibility makes the operating model easier to improve [S1].
