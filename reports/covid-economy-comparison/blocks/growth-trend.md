The chart shows the break clearly: growth was slowing before the pandemic, collapsed in 2020, overshot during reopening in 2021, and then settled into a slower range [S1].

The 2021 spike should not be read as a permanent improvement in the growth rate. It is partly a mathematical rebound from the 2020 low and partly the release of pent-up demand as restrictions eased.
