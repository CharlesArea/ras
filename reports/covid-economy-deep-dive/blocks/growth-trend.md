The series makes the phase change visible. The 2016–2019 values describe a positive but slowing baseline. The negative 2020 value marks the synchronized shock. The 2021 peak is the rebound. The 2022–2024 values show normalization, but at a pace below the long-run pre-pandemic average [S1].

The key interpretive caution is the difference between a level and a rate. A high growth rate in 2021 helped restore output after the 2020 fall, but it did not erase the lost activity, lost working hours, or uneven balance-sheet damage created by the shock. Nor does one strong year prove that the economy had returned to its previous trend.
