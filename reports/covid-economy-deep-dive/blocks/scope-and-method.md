This is a global report. It does not treat the experience of one country as a proxy for the whole economy, and it does not attempt to make country-specific claims where the source set is global. The purpose is to explain the direction and mechanism of change across the world economy.

| Phase | Years used here | Question answered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-COVID baseline | 2016–2019 | What was the economy doing before the shock? |
| Shock and first response | 2020 | How large was the break in output and work? |
| Rebound | 2021 | How quickly did activity return, and why was it uneven? |
| Adjustment | 2022 onward | What became different after reopening? |

The growth chart uses the IMF April 2024 statistical appendix so the displayed annual values come from one internally consistent vintage [S1]. The report combines that series with ILO labor-market evidence and IMF/World Bank analysis of inflation, recovery, and medium-term headwinds [S2][S3][S4].

Some conclusions in the later sections are analytical implications rather than direct measurements. They are labeled as interpretation and should not be read as country-level causal estimates.
